Industrial Vaastu / The Perspective

A factory is more than a building. It is an interconnected system.

Every industrial property brings together land, structures, machinery, materials, people and processes. In the Vaastu tradition, the orientation and arrangement of these elements are considered in relation to the purpose of the facility.

Dr. Ravi Rao provides Industrial Vaastu consultations tailored to the operational realities of each enterprise, combining traditional spatial principles with the practical requirements of industrial planning.
