Industrial Vaastu / The perspective

A factory is more than a building. It is an interconnected system.

Every industrial property brings together land, structures, machinery, materials, people and processes. In the Vaastu tradition, the orientation and arrangement of these elements are considered in relation to the purpose of the facility.

Dr. Ravi Rao provides Industrial Vaastu consultations tailored to the operational realities of each enterprise, combining traditional spatial principles with the practical requirements of industrial planning.

An important consideration

Plan early. Avoid costly changes later.

Industrial layouts involve substantial long-term investment. Considering Vaastu at the planning stage allows recommendations to be reviewed alongside architectural, safety and operational requirements — before construction and machinery placement make changes more difficult.

Before you get in touch

Can Vaastu be applied to an existing factory?

Yes. For existing units the consultation reviews the current arrangement and recommends practical adjustments where feasible.

Does it replace our architects and engineers?

No. Recommendations are coordinated with architectural, technical and safety requirements and with your project team.

When is the best time to involve Vaastu?

At the planning stage — site selection and master planning — before construction and machinery placement.
